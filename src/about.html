About Us

Brian Bullard

Brian Bullard is a small-town attorney in Faison, N.C. He earned his B.A. from U.N.C. Wilmington in 1993, his M.b.a. from Texas A&M University in 1996, and his J.d.d. from N.C. Central University in 1999. He has served as a prosecutor and District Attourney in Duplin County. Currently, he is a defense attorney for Duplin County.

When he's not at his office, he is the Scoutmaster of Faison's Scout Troop 48, he contributes to his college fraternity, Kappa Sigma, as an adult advisor. Sometimes, you'll even find him cheering on the Tar Heels in Kenan Stadium or the Dean Smith Center.
